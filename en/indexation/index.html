Indexing

The third step of the funnel — suggesting tags by content (coming soon).

This is the third and final step of the funnel: once your shots are selected, Indexing offers to tag them by content to prepare for the edit.

Coming soon

Indexing is under development. This page describes what it will do; it will grow once the feature is available.

What Indexing will do

Section titled “What Indexing will do”
  • Tags suggested by content: from what’s seen (local visual analysis), what’s heard and what’s said (speech transcription), and the metadata.
  • You validate, rename, discard: as everywhere in Varda, the suggestions are editable. It’s your vocabulary that structures your rushes, not the other way around.
  • Timelines colour-coded by tag, to find and group your shots.

Still local

Section titled “Still local”

Like the rest of Varda, the analysis and transcription will run on your machine — your rushes never go online.

What’s next

Section titled “What’s next”

The long-term goal: a complete export to DaVinci Resolve with dailies, colour-coded selects, and region and phrase markers — the funnel footage-logging method applied end to end, ready to edit.
